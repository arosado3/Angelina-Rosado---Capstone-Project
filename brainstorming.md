**Brain storm**

- StudySync - An app that allows students to post topics they need help with or topics they are capable of teaching. Students can connect and form study groups to improve learning.
- Pantry pal - keep track of expiration dates
- Hydration reminder - Press a single button when you drink water. This app keeps track of water intake and allows you to set goals. Also reminds the user to drink water
- Decision maker - User inputs a question with a series of choices. Then the user taps a button to spin the wheel and a random choice is automated.

**Top two:**
- Pantry pal
- Decision maker

**Evaluation:**
- Pantry pal:
    - Mobile: This app could include a camera to take a picture of certain items and obtain its expiration date via trends
    - Story: I think most people would use this app because it is really easy to forget about an item in your pantry and then be unable to use it.
    - Market: I feel like people who cook could benefit from this app. Additionally, I think this app could have a wide audience considering everyone on the planet commonly requires food to survive. So anyone who wanted to properly manage their pantry and save money by using each of their items before its expiration date could be interested in this app.
    - Habit: I feel like the average user would open this app weekly. They would have to add their items via camera or manually add the expiration date themselves. Then the app will remind them when the item expires.
    - Scope: I believe I could complete this app by the due date, however the camera aspect might be challenging to implement. 
- Decision maker:
    - Mobile: Could trigger haptics feedback as the wheel spins to mimic a real physical roulette wheel
        - Maybe also a shake to randomize features?
    - Story: I feel like this app would be really popular with indecisive people. I have seen a lot of couples use flashcards to decide on a place to go, so this app could be used instead as a substitution.
    - Market: I don't think this app has a well defined audience. Anyone could use it, but I envision indecisive individuals using this app more. 
    - Habit: The average person could open this app daily or once in a while. It depends on how indecisive they are.
        - The average user would have to add their own question and possible outcomes. 
    - Scope: I believe I could complete this app by its due date. I would be interested in building a stripped down version of this app.

**Final Decision**
- Decision maker
